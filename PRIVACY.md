**infetit radio privacy policy**

infetit radio stores your station settings, song drafts, lyrics, and saved recordings on your device. Playback and music generation run locally. infetit radio has no advertising, tracking, or developer-operated account system.

**Model downloads.** To create songs and use Radio, the app downloads the YuE2 music model from Hugging Face. Hugging Face receives the network information needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address. The model runs on your device after installation.

**Web Radio on Mac.** If you choose to start Web Radio, this Mac serves your station audio and playback information. If you make it public through a service such as Tailscale, listeners and that service receive the stream. If listener ideas are enabled, ideas submitted by listeners appear on this Mac and may be used in song generation. Stop hosting to end the stream.

**Songwriting.** You can supply your own lyrics or write on your device with Apple Intelligence or the optional Liquid AI model. Downloading the Liquid AI model connects to Hugging Face to retrieve model files. Your songwriting text stays on your device when you use this model.

**Optional Gemini.** If you turn on Gemini in Songwriting settings and save an API key, infetit radio sends songwriting prompts and draft text to Google to generate, fit, or revise lyrics and descriptions. Testing a key sends a short test prompt. The API key is stored in your device’s Keychain and sent to Google for authentication. Google’s terms govern its handling of these requests. Turn off Gemini or remove the key in Songwriting settings to stop future requests. [Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)

**Apple Music.** If you ask to find a released song, infetit radio sends the search terms to Apple. Apple handles playback and account activity in Apple Music under its own policies. [Apple privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

Your Library and Songwriting settings let you delete saved songs or remove the local songwriter download. Data already received by Google or Apple is subject to that provider’s retention and deletion policies.

For privacy questions, contact the developer through [infetit radio support](https://github.com/ferdinandl007/infetit-radio/issues). Avoid including API keys, private prompts, or recordings in public issues.
